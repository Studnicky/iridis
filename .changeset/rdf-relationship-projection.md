RDF reasoning graphs project authored relationship graphs, exact operation parameters, tolerances, and resolved-role relationship provenance for queryable forward and reverse derivation traversal. `hue-target` relationships emit `iridis:hueTarget` and `iridis:hueClamp` as `xsd:double` literals. RDF consumers query derivation through relationship graph resources; role-level `iridis:derivedFrom`, `iridis:hueClamp`, and `iridis:hueOffset` predicates are not emitted.
