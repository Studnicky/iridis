Failing pairs in the contrast:complianceReport carry an optional `proof` that states whether the pair is reachable at the run's resolved hues, and `analyzeSchema` reports the same reachability for a schema before any run. `analyzeSchema` certifies neutral envelopes and bounded authored hue arcs with conservative luminance witnesses. A pair whose hue is not fixed is reported as indeterminate with its sampled evidence kept separate from the proof.
