The contrast:complianceReport is `{compliant, disposition, selected, pairs, problemPairs}`, where `disposition` is resolved, best-effort or withheld and `problemPairs` is the failing subset of `pairs`. Each pair carries required, actual, shortfall, adjustedRoles and constraints, where constraints are pin, range, hue-relationship, gamut, cvd-floor or cvd-drop. input.contrast.enableStrictCompliance marks an unresolved report withheld. Connected roles are optimized jointly inside their authored ranges.
